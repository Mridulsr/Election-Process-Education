Election Process Education

Your Vote Matters

Understanding the electoral process is the first step to active citizenship.

Why We Vote

Voting is the foundation of democracy, allowing citizens to have a direct voice in how their government operates and who represents them.

Secure Process

Modern elections use multiple layers of security, including voter verification, secure paper trails, and public audits.

The Voting Process

A step-by-step guide to how an election is conducted.

1

Voter Registration

Citizens must register to vote before an election. This step verifies eligibility and assigns voters to the correct polling location based on their residence.

2

Casting the Ballot

Voters can typically cast their ballots early, by mail, or in-person on Election Day. Most jurisdictions use secure paper ballots or machines that generate a paper record.

3

Tabulation

Ballots are securely collected and counted. Optical scanners process paper ballots rapidly, while maintaining the physical paper trail for potential audits.

4

Certification

After all valid votes are counted and any mandatory audits or recounts are completed, election officials formally certify the results, making them official.
